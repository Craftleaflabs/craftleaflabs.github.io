Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026 · Last updated 29 September 2026

The short version

  • You don’t need an account to play, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress, coins and settings are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device’s advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads.
  • We don’t sell your personal information.

Who we are

Letterport (“the app”) is a word puzzle game for Android published by Craftleaf Labs, operated by Muzammil Saleem (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies only to the Letterport app.

Information saved on your device

To remember where you are in the game, the app saves the following on your phone:

  • levels completed, current level and letters you have revealed
  • coins, bonus words found and passport stamps
  • daily puzzle progress, streak and daily reward day
  • your sound and music settings

This information stays on your device. We don’t collect it, can’t see it, and don’t share it. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Letterport is free because it shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. Some ads are optional “rewarded” videos you can choose to watch for coins or a free hint; others may appear between levels.

To show and measure ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language and app version
  • how you interact with ads, for example whether an ad was shown or tapped
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. On supported devices, Google may also use Android’s Privacy Sandbox features (such as ad topics and attribution) for these purposes. We do not receive your advertising ID or other personal data from Google in a way that identifies you.

Learn more about how Google uses this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Personalized ads: on most Android phones, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. Without it, ads are not personalized to you.
  • Privacy Sandbox: on Android 13 and later you can manage ad topics and related settings under Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads privacy.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice later from the app’s settings.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Purchases

The app does not currently sell anything. If we add in-app purchases in the future, payments will be handled entirely by Google Play. We will never receive your card or bank details.

Children

Letterport is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

Sharing and selling

We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t share it with anyone for their own marketing. The only third party that processes data through the app is Google, for the advertising described above.

Security and how long data is kept

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s own retention policies. Google transfers data over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in Europe and the CCPA/CPRA in California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to or limit how it is used. Because we don’t hold personal information about you, most requests can be handled directly on your phone using the ad settings above. For information held by Google, see Google’s data and privacy controls. You can also contact us, and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding purchases or new features, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s update notes on Google Play.
